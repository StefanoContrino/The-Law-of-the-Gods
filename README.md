# Terraria Mod: The Law of the Gods

A Terraria mod inspired by Lovecraftian horror and cosmic mysteries, aiming to expand Terraria's lore by introducing a darker atmosphere, deeper storytelling, and new horrors to discover.

This mod will add a large amount of new content, alongside a structured lore that players can uncover throughout their journey.

Current planned features:

* 2 new Town NPCs
  * Charles
  * Zadok
* 30 new weapons for each class
* 17 new enemies, 8 in Pre-Hardmode and 9 in Hardmode
* 3 new biomes: 2 new surface biomes, one of which also generates underground, and 1 space biome
  * Innsmouth
  * The Consecrated Land
  * The Floating Ruins of R'lyeh
* A new Snow Temple to expand the existing biome
* 4 new bosses inspired by Lovecraft's works, 2 in Pre-Hardmode and 2 in Hardmode
  * The King in Yellow
  * The Shoggoth Prime
  * Mother Hydra
  * Yuggoth
* Hundreds of new items, including blocks, gadgets, lore items, and miscellaneous content
* A custom soundtrack for new structures and biomes

## Lore

### The Age of Darkness
The apocalypse was supposed to end evil. Instead, it let evil win.

For thousands of years, cosmic horrors and ancient gods have ruled the Earth. They twisted the land into a nightmare of flesh, stone, and madness. Humanity wasn’t just defeated; it was erased. Every city, every memory, every soul was consumed or driven into extinction. The world that remains is a graveyard. The ruins of the old order are buried beneath layers of corruption and forgotten terror.

### The Last Survivor
Against all odds, one man endured: the Guide. He wasn’t a hero or a god. He was just the last human alive, a witness to the end of everything. Through cunning, sacrifice, and maybe a fragment of divine favor, he managed to evade the clutches of the old gods. He hid in the shadows of a world that no longer belonged to him.

For millennia, he watched, learned, and waited. He studied the enemies that had destroyed humanity, uncovering their weaknesses and the forbidden knowledge they guarded.

### The Creation
Time alone couldn’t undo what had been done. The Guide knew he couldn’t defeat the cosmic horrors by himself. He needed a successor.

Using a fragment of his own DNA and ancient technologies salvaged from the ruins of the old world, he engineered a new being: you.

You are not just a copy. You are an evolution. Your mind holds the Guide's knowledge, your hands carry his skills, but your very structure has been rewritten. You can absorb, adapt, and wield powers that would drive a normal human insane. Mutations, cosmic insights, and forbidden abilities flow through you, gifts and curses forged in the desperation of a dying species.

### The Mission
Your purpose is singular: hunt the old gods and their minions, and reclaim the world from eternal darkness.

The Guide has set you upon a path paved with blood and madness. Along the way, you will uncover the truth of the apocalypse, the origins of the cosmic horrors, and the price that must be paid to undo what was done.

But beware: power comes at a cost. The same forces that grant you strength may also consume your sanity. The line between hunter and hunted is thinner than you think. In a world where the gods are real and they are hungry, survival is not guaranteed, even for a created messiah.

### The Lovecraftian Connection
This mod expands on Terraria's subtle hints of cosmic dread, weaving a narrative deeply inspired by H.P. Lovecraft. The enemies you face are not mere monsters. They are manifestations of ancient, incomprehensible entities that existed before humanity and will remain long after. The biomes you explore are twisted by their influence, and the items you find are remnants of civilizations that dared to look too deeply into the void.

The question isn't whether you can win. The question is: what will you become in the process?
